# Decisions and Rationale

1. **Knowledge-base first:** Validate organic demand before substantial app investment.
2. **B1/B2 starting focus:** Broad relevance, with DS-160 and procedural issue subtopics. Other visa categories later.
3. **Four launch markets:** Pakistan first; India, Bangladesh and Philippines next. Other language markets only with adequate verification.
4. **Problem-resolution differentiator:** Generic FAQ answers are not enough. Future structured pathways should identify the user's stage and send them to verified next actions, not speculate about immigration outcomes.
5. **Static first:** Astro/TypeScript with Markdown/MDX avoids paid CMS, database and infrastructure complexity.
6. **Free-first budget:** Founder wants almost no *additional* cash cost; use existing ChatGPT access and free Claude / Codex capabilities within limits. Do not assume subscriptions include free API usage.
7. **AI-assisted, human-approved:** AI drafts/researches/builds; founder approves publication and critical changes. Qualified professionals may be necessary for personalized legal advice.
8. **Public repository:** Website source and published public reference material may be public; protect secrets, applicant data and commercially sensitive research.
9. **No approval scoring:** Do not output invented probabilities or imply VisaOS can affect decisions made by consular officers.
10. **Measured growth:** SEO/GEO/AEO plans are hypotheses until Search Console and keyword tools show results; no ranking or traffic guarantees.
11. **Dependency-free CSS retained:** The initial Tailwind proposal is superseded. Continue the existing global CSS system unless a measured accessibility, maintainability or product requirement demonstrates a need for another system.
12. **Collection publishing is deny-by-default:** Content entries generate public routes only when founder-approved and explicitly indexable. Draft, pending-review, rejected and non-indexable entries remain absent from the production build, sitemap and Pagefind index.
13. **Production origin confirmed:** Public metadata and crawl-control files use `https://visaos.devshinx.dev`. Cloudflare Pages and DNS changes remain manual, approval-gated operations.
