# 12-Week Roadmap (Targets, Not Guarantees)

1. Week 1: 200–300 initial keyword candidates and first-20-page plan; use measured search data when available.
2. Week 2: Astro foundation, reusable templates, sitemap/SEO and CI.
3. Week 3: Source registry + five to eight reviewed initial pages.
4. Week 4: Public launch with ten to twelve approved pages, Search Console and analytics.
5. Week 5: Country hub pages across four initial markets.
6. Week 6: DS-160 question and troubleshooting cluster.
7. Week 7: Privacy-preserving client-side Visa Issue Navigator prototype.
8. Week 8: Common operational issue pages, 25–30 total approved pages as stretch target.
9. Week 9: Technical SEO audit and one original useful resource.
10. Week 10: Search Console data analysis and real keyword opportunities.
11. Week 11: Refresh winners, consolidate weak/overlapping pages.
12. Week 12: Review 30–40 pages as stretch goal, organic visibility, tool usage and next 90-day decision.

Do not substitute volume for verification. Expanding to more source countries or a paid product is gated by evidence and maintenance capacity.
