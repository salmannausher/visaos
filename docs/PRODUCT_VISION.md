# VisaOS Product Vision

VisaOS is a trustworthy, independent guide to navigating visa applications. Start with U.S. nonimmigrant visa information (especially B1/B2 visitor visas), with global audiences and country-of-application context. Long-term ambition: an integrated Visa Intelligence and Journey Management Platform with personalized roadmaps, DS-160 preparation, source-backed problem diagnosis, document consistency checks, case tracking, and selective qualified-human escalation.

## Current product wedge
The first product is a *public visa knowledge base*, not a paid case-management SaaS. Use search traffic and engagement to validate demand before building personalized tools. Differentiation: precise, contextual answers linked to official sources; understandable decision trees for administrative problems; useful tools; ongoing maintenance.

## Audience and scope
Initial source-country content: Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Philippines. Later research/conditional expansion: Vietnam, Indonesia, Brazil, Colombia. Country of nationality, country of residence, and consular application post are different dimensions; do not conflate them.

## Product values
Accurate over fast; helpful over keyword-stuffed; transparent about uncertainty; protect user privacy; no implied government affiliation, legal credentials or guarantees of approval.
