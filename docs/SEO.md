# SEO / GEO / AEO Strategy

Optimize for discoverable, helpful information and genuine user value. Do not claim there is an AI-specific ranking shortcut.

## Market rollout
Wave 1: Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Philippines, starting English content. Wave 2 conditional: Vietnam/Indonesia with competent native-language editorial checks. Wave 3 conditional: Brazil/Colombia with Portuguese/Spanish editorial checks.

## Keyword research
Record query, country, language, intent, topic cluster, estimated volume *with source*, SERP competitors and URLs, differentiation, editorial cost, ranking hypothesis and business relevance. Do not fabricate keyword volumes, rankings or difficulty scores. Early query candidate lists are not measurements.

## Content architecture
Universal pillar pages, country-specific procedural pages, question pages, and problem-resolution pages. Avoid duplicate thin pages changing only country name. One page per distinct search intent; supportive internal linking. Lead with direct answer, then applicability/conditions, verified steps, caveats, official source links, related content and useful navigation.

## SEO foundations
Indexable static HTML, sitemap, canonical, metadata, hreflang only for real translations, mobile performance, JSON-LD appropriate to page type, clean navigation, helpful about/editorial pages. Track Search Console indexing, impressions, non-brand clicks, queries and pages. In GA4 measure tool starts/completions and qualified signup interest.

## Outcome interpretation
First 90 days: validate execution, indexing, initial impressions/engagement, not promise top positions. Six-month review to choose winning country and topic clusters. Example targets (hypotheses only): 30–40 quality pages by week 12, ~20 indexable/indexed pages, 1k monthly impressions, 50 clicks, 25 tool starts; update from actual data.
