# Sprint 5 Evidence and Founder Editorial Review

Prepared 2026-10-29. This is a research and review package, not a publication approval. All five new entries remain `editorialStatus: draft` and `noindex: true`; none creates a production route, sitemap entry or Pagefind record.

## Evidence method and limits

- Every source below was opened directly on an official U.S. Department of State website on 2026-10-29. Search snippets, vendor pages, forums and remembered rules were not used as authority.
- The repository still has no measured keyword volume, difficulty, ranking position or localized SERP audit for these intents. Those fields remain `UNKNOWN`, `UNVERIFIED`, `NOT AUDITED` or `PENDING` in the Sprint 3 handoff.
- Global Department guidance does not establish every post-specific workflow. Where an embassy, consulate or scheduling system controls the next step, the drafts say so instead of inventing a universal procedure.
- Refusal pages explain published Department guidance, not an individual legal conclusion. Founder review should consider independent U.S. immigration-law review before publication.
- The paid expedited-appointment pilot is temporary and high-volatility. Its locations, fee, inventory, deadline and interface must be rechecked immediately before any publication decision.
