## Evidence method and limits

- Every source below was opened directly on an official U.S. Department of State website on 2026-10-29. Search snippets, vendor pages, forums and remembered rules were not used as authority.
- The repository still has no measured keyword volume, difficulty, ranking position or localized SERP audit for these intents. Those fields remain `UNKNOWN`, `UNVERIFIED`, `NOT AUDITED` or `PENDING` in the Sprint 3 handoff.
- Global Department guidance does not establish every post-specific workflow. Where an embassy, consulate or scheduling system controls the next step, the drafts say so instead of inventing a universal procedure.
- Refusal pages explain published Department guidance, not an individual legal conclusion. Founder review should consider independent U.S. immigration-law review before publication.
- The paid expedited-appointment pilot is temporary and high-volatility. Its locations, fee, inventory, deadline and interface must be rechecked immediately before any publication decision.

Controlled publication recheck on 2026-10-30 confirmed that the DS-160 FAQ, DS-160 overview and CEAC endpoint still loaded. The FAQ still used `Option C — Retrieve Application`; the live CEAC landing page displayed `RETRIEVE AN APPLICATION` and stated that an application ID is needed to retrieve the application. No universal missing-ID recovery procedure was published in the reviewed global guidance.

The recheck found one precision issue and three evidence boundaries:

1. **Corrected:** the paid pilot source describes a US$750 charge for each applicant and, in its overview, as a fee per appointment. The draft now uses “for each applicant” and retains the group-applicant qualification from the official FAQ.
2. **Unresolved:** the DS-160 FAQ requires an application ID for the documented confirmation-page reprint route but does not publish a universal lost-ID recovery flow. The draft now makes that stopping point prominent and does not promise that a post can recover the ID.
3. **Unresolved:** the Department's submitted-DS-160 reopening instructions address an officer-directed correction after refusal. The global pages do not establish one pre-interview replacement, barcode-linking or rescheduling procedure.
4. **Volatile:** the paid pilot currently lists six participating missions/posts and an end date of December 31, 2026, while warning that additional locations may be announced. All locations, dates, fees and availability require a same-day publication recheck.

No unsupported approval guarantee, appointment guarantee, processing-time promise or universal post procedure was found after the revisions. The 214(b) and 221(g) legal framing remains unapproved pending independent review.
