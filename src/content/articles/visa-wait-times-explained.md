The Department of State publishes a [global visa wait-time table](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/global-visa-wait-times.html) with figures that vary by application location and visa category. The page is updated over time, so use the official table rather than a copied number.

## Average wait and next available appointment are different

The official page distinguishes historical average wait times from an estimate for the next available interview appointment. A historical average describes what applicants typically experienced during a stated earlier period. A next-available estimate describes current scheduling availability. The two figures can differ.

## A published figure is not a guarantee

The Department of State explicitly warns that reported averages do not guarantee an appointment within a particular period. Posts can add appointments, availability can change, and post-specific instructions control how an applicant uses the scheduling system.

## Check category, post and update date

Before interpreting a number, confirm the city or post, the visa-category column and the page's update date. Do not apply a B-1/B-2 figure to a different category or a figure from one post to another. VisaOS does not monitor appointment accounts or promise earlier appointments.
