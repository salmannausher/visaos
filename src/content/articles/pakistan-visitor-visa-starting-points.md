> **Editorial draft — not approved for publication.** The U.S. Mission Pakistan source returned an automated-access error during research and remains marked `needs-review`. A human editor must open and verify the current local instructions before approval.

The Department of State's [visitor visa overview](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/tourism-visit/visitor.html) is the global starting point for B-1, B-2 and combined B-1/B-2 classifications. It also says that the order and method of application steps can vary by embassy or consulate.

## Local instructions must be checked directly

Before this guide can be published, an editor must verify the current [U.S. Mission Pakistan visa information](https://pk.usembassy.gov/visas/) and any scheduling provider linked from that official government page. Until that review is complete, this draft intentionally does not state local fee-payment steps, appointment procedures, courier arrangements, interview-waiver rules or document lists.

## Keep country dimensions separate

Pakistani nationality, residence in Pakistan and applying at a post in Pakistan are not interchangeable facts. A final guide must state which dimension controls each instruction and must not imply that one local procedure applies to every Pakistani citizen worldwide.

## What can be verified now

Applicants can use the Department of State page to understand the general visitor visa classifications. Operational steps should come from the current official instructions for the actual application post. VisaOS cannot book an appointment, access an account or influence a visa decision.
