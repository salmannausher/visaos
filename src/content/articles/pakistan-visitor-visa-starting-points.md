The Department of State's [visitor visa overview](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/tourism-visit/visitor.html) is the global starting point for B-1, B-2 and combined B-1/B-2 classifications. Applicants whose cases will be handled in Pakistan should also follow the current [U.S. Mission Pakistan nonimmigrant visa instructions](https://pk.usembassy.gov/nonimmigrant-visas/). Local procedures and interview policies can change, so use those official pages rather than an old checklist or an unofficial booking service.

## Start with the purpose of travel

B-1 generally covers qualifying temporary business activities. B-2 generally covers tourism, visits and certain other temporary personal purposes. A combined B-1/B-2 visa can cover qualifying activities in either category, but it does not permit ordinary U.S. employment, degree study or permanent residence. See the fuller VisaOS [B-1, B-2 and B-1/B-2 guide](/us/visitor-visa/) and confirm the classification against the Department of State page.

## Follow the official Pakistan process

The Mission's nonimmigrant visa page directs applicants to complete the DS-160, pay the applicable application fee and schedule an interview through the provider linked from the Mission's own website. Start from the `.gov` page so that a copied or sponsored link does not send you to an unofficial service.

Use the Mission's [Nonimmigrant Visa Navigator](https://pk.usembassy.gov/nonimmigrant-visa-navigator/) for current Pakistan-specific resources and inquiries. VisaOS does not collect fees, schedule appointments, access applicant accounts or provide expedited access.

## Interview rules and timing can change

The Mission publishes current information about interviews, possible exceptions and urgent-travel requests. Eligibility for any exception depends on the current policy and the applicant's facts; it should not be inferred from age, a previous visa or an older web post alone. Check the official page immediately before paying or scheduling.

Appointment availability and administrative processing are not guaranteed. Do not use a copied wait-time figure to make non-refundable travel plans. The Department of State maintains a separate [global wait-time page](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/global-visa-wait-times.html), and VisaOS explains [how to interpret its figures](/us/visa-wait-times/).

## Documents and evidence are case-specific

The general Department of State visitor visa page lists baseline items and explains that additional evidence may be requested. The Mission Pakistan page likewise warns that there is not one universal supporting-document list for every nonimmigrant applicant. Follow the instructions for the visa category and post, and bring truthful evidence relevant to the purpose of travel and personal circumstances. VisaOS cannot determine eligibility or influence a visa decision.

## Keep country dimensions separate

Pakistani nationality, residence in Pakistan and applying at a post in Pakistan are not interchangeable facts. This page addresses applications handled through U.S. Mission Pakistan. A Pakistani citizen applying in another country must follow the official instructions for that application post.
