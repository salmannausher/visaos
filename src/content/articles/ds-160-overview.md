The DS-160 is the Department of State's online nonimmigrant visa application. The Department says the form is submitted electronically and that consular officers use the information in visa processing. Start with the official [DS-160 overview and access instructions](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/forms/ds-160-online-nonimmigrant-visa-application.html).

## Submission is one part of the process

Submitting the form does not complete every visa step. The Department of State directs applicants to keep the barcode confirmation page, consult the relevant embassy or consulate for country-specific instructions, arrange any required interview through the proper channel and follow current fee instructions.

The sequence can depend on the visa category and application post. Use the [official DS-160 frequently asked questions](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/forms/ds-160-online-nonimmigrant-visa-application/ds-160-faqs.html) together with the website of the embassy or consulate handling the application.

## Accuracy matters

The official FAQ tells applicants to answer the form accurately and completely. VisaOS cannot choose answers for an applicant, access a submitted form or correct a government record. If a question depends on personal legal circumstances, use an official channel or qualified professional rather than guessing.

## Privacy reminder

Do not post a DS-160 application ID, confirmation page, passport data or answers in a public issue, repository or forum. VisaOS does not need those details to explain the public process.
