The U.S. Department of State describes visitor visas as nonimmigrant visas for temporary travel. It distinguishes temporary business travel under B-1, tourism or visits under B-2, and a combined B-1/B-2 classification. Review the Department of State's [current visitor visa overview](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/tourism-visit/visitor.html) before relying on those labels.

## Start with the real purpose of the trip

The visa classification should match the genuine purpose of travel. The official overview gives examples of activities associated with B-1 and B-2, but examples are not a personalized eligibility decision. Employment, degree study and permanent residence are among the purposes the Department of State identifies as requiring a different route.

If the purpose is unclear or involves several activities, use the Department of State's [visa category starting point](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas.html) and seek qualified advice for an individualized legal question.

## Instructions depend on the application location

The Department of State says the order and method of application steps may vary by embassy or consulate. After identifying the likely classification, consult the official website for the location where the interview or application will be handled. Do not substitute another country's scheduling instructions or a forum checklist.

## What this draft does not decide

This page does not determine eligibility, predict approval, provide a universal document checklist or replace instructions from the relevant U.S. embassy or consulate.
